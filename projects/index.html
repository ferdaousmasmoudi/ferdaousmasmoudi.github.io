Research Pillars

Structured around complete systems, from field deployment and embedded intelligence to digital infrastructure and energy autonomy.

🤖

Autonomous Robotics for Agricultural Environments

Design and control of autonomous and semi-autonomous mobile robots for farming contexts, including navigation, obstacle avoidance, environmental perception, and multi-sensor fusion.

  • Functions: autonomous driving, teleoperation, sensor-rich navigation
  • Sensing: IMU, LiDAR, vision, gas, humidity, environmental monitoring
  • Focus: robust field robotics for real agricultural environments
🧠

AI-Driven Embedded Systems & Intelligent Decision-Making

Development of embedded and distributed AI architectures for edge inference, task execution, perception pipelines, and connected decision-support systems.

  • Execution: edge AI, cloud-assisted intelligence, real-time processing
  • Methods: computer vision, embedded inference, task-level intelligence
  • Goal: make smart devices operational in constrained environments
🌾

Smart Farming & Agriculture 5.0 Platforms

Integration of robotics, connected sensing, AI, and cyber-physical supervision into next-generation agricultural systems and digital farming platforms.

  • Scope: Agriculture 5.0, digital farms, cyber-physical integration
  • Architecture: field devices, cloud dashboards, mobile interaction
  • Value: intelligent monitoring, automation, and operational visibility
📡

Full-Stack IoT System Design

End-to-end design of IoT systems covering embedded firmware, electronics, custom boards, wireless communication, cloud services, and user-facing dashboards.

  • Engineering: PCB design, firmware, sensor integration, device orchestration
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi, Zigbee, LoRa, BLE, GSM, RFID
  • Approach: complete stack from hardware to software interface
🕹️

Immersive & Interactive Technologies

Design of interactive embedded devices and connected systems for user-centered experiences, therapeutic robotics, and gamified physical computing environments.

  • Orientation: human-centered systems and interactive device design
  • Applications: therapeutic robotics, immersive environments, smart interaction
  • Logic: connect hardware behavior with meaningful user experience
🔗

Blockchain-Based Traceability for Agro-Food Systems

Secure traceability architectures linking field operations, operator actions, geolocation, timestamps, IoT data, QR/NFC layers, and cloud dashboards.

  • Systems: blockchain-backed product history and workflow traceability
  • Interfaces: QR, NFC, mobile access, dashboard supervision
  • Compliance: GS1, ISO, HACCP-oriented digital traceability logic
☀️

Renewable Energy Integration

Optimization and control of photovoltaic systems for powering embedded devices, robotics platforms, and digital infrastructures in isolated or resource-constrained zones.

  • Topics: PV modeling, MPPT, storage, energy-aware control
  • Use Case: autonomous power supply for embedded and field systems
  • Positioning: energy as an enabling layer for intelligent systems
