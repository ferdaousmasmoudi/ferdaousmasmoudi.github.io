# PhD Thesis: Optimization of Standalone Photovoltaic Systems

**Defended on**: December 31, 2016  
**Institution**: ENIS Sfax  

## Research Overview  
This thesis focuses on the optimization of standalone photovoltaic systems, addressing key challenges in efficiency and reliability. The research includes a comprehensive study of energy requirements, environmental impacts, and the integration of innovative technologies to maximize output.

## Key Contributions  
1. Development of a novel algorithm for energy management in standalone systems.  
2. Comprehensive modeling of photovoltaic system performance under varying environmental conditions.  
3. Implementation of real-world case studies to validate optimization techniques.
